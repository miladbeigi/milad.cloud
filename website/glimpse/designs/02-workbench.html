Glimpse v1.6.0 · macOS menu bar
A native Mac app for showing your screen

Grab the screen.Draw the point.Hand it to a person, or your agent.

Glimpse is one menu bar app for everything you show and tell from your screen. Capture it, mark it up, send it to a person or to Claude Code and Codex, and find it again later. Agents can use it too.

  • Free, MIT
  • macOS 14+
  • Apple silicon and Intel
  • On-device OCR and transcripts
NOW ON SCREEN · EDITOR + RECENT STACK
01
Station one · 7 ways in

Capture

Freeze the screen and take exactly the part you mean: an area, a window, a whole page, or just its text.

  • ⌥⇧⌘4AreaFrozen screen, magnifier, live size
  • ⌥⇧⌘5WindowClean cut-out
  • ⌥⇧⌘6Scrolling pageThe whole thing, top to bottom
  • ⌥⇧⌘2TextOn-device OCR, or QR
  • ⌥⇧⌘3Fullscreen
  • ⌥⇧⌘7Previous areaSame rectangle again
  • ⌥⇧⌘8Self-timer

03
Station three · 4 exits

Hand off

Give it to a person, or press ⌘↩ and give it to Claude Code or Codex.

  • Quick Access OverlayWaits in the corner. Copy or save.
  • Drag anywhereInto Slack, Mail or Finder
  • PinFloats above every window
  • ⌘↩ Send to your agentPick a session when several are open
04
Station four · 3 shelves

Keep

Double-tap right Shift and your last 30 screenshots slide in at the edge of the screen.

  • ⇧⇧ · ⌥⇧⌘0Recent stackYour last 30, select several at once
  • StitchOne numbered imageLaid out so an AI model shrinks it the least
  • ~/Glimpse/MeetingsMeeting notesEach call becomes a markdown file
05
Station five · 6 abilities

Agents

It works both ways. An MCP server lets Claude Code, Cursor and other agents see your screen and show you things. It stays off until you allow it.

  • listList open windows
  • screenshotScreenshot one windowEven when it is covered
  • readRead the text on screen
  • recordRecord short clipsTo check their own work
  • drawDraw in their own colourArrows, boxes, notes to show or ask you
  • searchSearch meeting transcripts
# once
$ claude mcp add glimpse -- /Applications/Glimpse.app/Contents/MacOS/Glimpse mcp

# later, in a session
› Check the chart after your change. Mark anything that looks off.
glimpse · list windows
glimpse · screenshot "Northwind"
glimpse · draw box + note "16 pt gap here"
claude mcp add glimpse -- /Applications/Glimpse.app/Contents/MacOS/Glimpse mcp
06
Station six · recordings

Meetings

Record the screen, or let Glimpse notice a call and keep an on-device transcript with speakers.

  • ⌥⇧⌘RRecord the screenMP4 with camera bubble, mic and system audio
  • ⌃⌥MMute the micWhile recording
  • 8 appsCall detectionMeet, Zoom, Teams, Slack, Discord, FaceTime, Webex, Tuple
  • On-deviceTranscript with speakersVoice profiles never leave the Mac
