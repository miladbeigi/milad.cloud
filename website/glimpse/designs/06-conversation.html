Show your screen to your coding agent. It can look, and show you back.

Glimpse is one native Mac app for screenshots, screen recordings and meeting notes. It sends what you capture to Claude Code or Codex in one key, and gives your agent its own eyes through MCP.

Everything else it does, for people.

No agent needed. Glimpse is a full screenshot tool, recorder and notetaker on its own, living in the menu bar.

01

Capture any shape

The screen freezes while you pick, with a magnifier and live size.

  • Area ⌥⇧⌘4
  • Window, cut out ⌥⇧⌘5
  • Scrolling page ⌥⇧⌘6
  • Text via OCR, QR ⌥⇧⌘2
  • Fullscreen ⌥⇧⌘3
  • Previous area ⌥⇧⌘7
  • Self-timer ⌥⇧⌘8
02

Mark it up

Every mark stays editable. Curved arrows, boxes, numbered steps, highlighter, text, notes, pixelate, blur, spotlight, crop. One key each.

03

It waits in the corner

Copy, save, or drag it into Slack, Mail or Finder. ⌥⇧⌘9 brings back the last one.

04

Pin above everything

Keep a reference on top of every window while you work.

05

Recent stack and Stitch

⇧⇧ or ⌥⇧⌘0 shows your last 30. Stitch several into one numbered image, laid out so an AI model shrinks it the least.

Two commands.

The first installs Glimpse. The second lets Claude Code use it. Cursor and other MCP clients use the same binary.

1 · install the app
$curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/miladbeigi/glimpse/master/install.sh | bash
2 · give your agent eyes
$claude mcp add glimpse -- /Applications/Glimpse.app/Contents/MacOS/Glimpse mcp

Or download the latest release. Source on GitHub.

  • priceFree and open source, MIT
  • runs onmacOS 14 or later, Apple silicon and Intel
  • privacyOCR, transcripts and voice profiles never leave the Mac
