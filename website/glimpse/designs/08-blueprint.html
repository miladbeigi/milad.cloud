CAPTUREDWG 01

01Area capture

Press ⌥⇧⌘4 and the screen freezes, so menus and hover states stay put while you choose. A magnifier shows the pixels under the cursor, a label shows the live size.

Window, scrolling page, text, fullscreen, previous area and self-timer work the same way, each on its own key.

AGENTSDWG 10

10MCP server and glimpse:// URLs

It works both ways. Agents can list windows, screenshot one even when covered, read text, record short clips to check their own work, draw on screenshots in their own colour to show or ask you something, and search meeting transcripts.

Off until you allow it in Settings › Agents. Recording has its own switch.

Connect

claude mcp add glimpse -- /Applications/Glimpse.app/Contents/MacOS/Glimpse mcp

Script it

open "glimpse://capture-area?x=100&y=80&width=800&height=400"

For Shortcuts, Raycast and scripts.
