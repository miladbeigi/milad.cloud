Glimpse for macOS

Show your screen. To people, and to your agent.

One native menu bar app to capture, mark up, hand off and keep everything you show from your screen. Free and open source.

Grab exactly what you mean.

The screen freezes. A magnifier and a live size readout help you land on the pixel.

04 · Mark up

Every mark stays editable.

Curved arrows, boxes, numbered steps, highlighter, text, pixelate, blur, spotlight, crop. Notes land beside a box or arrow as you type.

05 · Hand off

It waits in the corner.

Every capture lands in the corner of your screen. Copy it, save it, or drag it straight into Slack, Mail or Finder.

Bring back the last one ⌥⇧⌘9

06 · Pin

Pin it above everything.

Keep a design, a spec or a number floating over every window while you work underneath it.

07 · Send

⌘↩ sends it to your agent.

Straight into Claude Code or Codex. With several sessions open, a menu lists them. The Claude Code plugin delivers it between turns.

08 · Record

Record the screen. And yourself.

MP4 with a camera bubble, your mic and the system audio. Pause, restart or stop from a small bar.

Record ⌥⇧⌘RMute mic ⌃⌥M

09 · Meetings

Meetings, written down.

Glimpse notices calls in Meet, Zoom, Teams, Slack, Discord, FaceTime, Webex and Tuple. You get a transcript with speakers, made on your Mac, saved as markdown in ~/Glimpse/Meetings.

10 · Keep

Your last 30, one tap away.

Double-tap right Shift and recent captures slide in at the screen edge. Select a few to copy, draw on, send or stitch.

⇧⇧

11 · Stitch

Many shots. One image.

Stitch numbers them and lays them out so an AI model shrinks the result the least.

12 · Agents

It works both ways.

Through MCP, Claude Code, Cursor and others can list windows, screenshot one even when covered, read text, record a short clip to check their work, draw on screenshots in their own colour, and search meeting transcripts. Off until you allow it.

claude mcp add glimpse -- /Applications/Glimpse.app/Contents/MacOS/Glimpse mcp

14 · Install

Get Glimpse.

Free. macOS 14 and later, Apple silicon and Intel. It updates itself. glimpse:// URLs work from Shortcuts, Raycast and scripts.

Install in Terminal
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/miladbeigi/glimpse/master/install.sh | bash
Then connect your agent (optional)
claude mcp add glimpse -- /Applications/Glimpse.app/Contents/MacOS/Glimpse mcp
